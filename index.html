The Hearthside Heritage

Born from a Love of Craft & Community

Founded in 2018 in an authentic cobblestone district warehouse, Aroma & Hearth was conceived as an antidote to frantic quick-service café culture. We envisioned a place where time slows, where the crackle of wood fire and the rich aroma of Ethiopian washed coffees create an unhurried sanctuary.

Every bean is roasted on-site in micro-batches using our restored 1968 cast-iron Probat roaster. Every loaf of sourdough ferments undisturbed for 36 hours before hitting the refractory stone oven floor at dawn. We do not take shortcuts, because the deepest flavors demand patience.

Our Devotion

The Pillars of Aroma & Hearth

We believe authentic hospitality lies in intentional materials, ethical relationships, and zero culinary compromise.

agriculture

Farm-Direct Ingredients

From Hudson Valley pastured dairy to local mill stoneground flours, our supply chain is transparent, regenerative, and deeply rooted in small-farm stewardship.

fire_hydrant

Micro-Batch Roasting

We roast in small 5kg batches on our restored 1968 Probat. This allows our roastmaster to monitor temperature curves by the second for optimal sweetness.

fireplace

Living Hearth Atmosphere

Real wood-burning stone fireplaces, reclaimed pine tabletops, acoustic treatment for quiet conversations, and soft curated ambient jazz vinyl.

sentiment_very_satisfied

Warm & Attentive Service

No pretension. Our baristas delight in guiding newcomers through roast origins, recommending bespoke pairings, and greeting regulars by name.

speed

Fiber Wi-Fi & Work Benches

Need to craft your next project? Enjoy dedicated quiet work tables with wireless charging spots, abundant discreet plugs, and unmetered gigabit fiber internet.

compost

Zero Food Waste Mandate

Surplus daily hearth bakes are donated nightly to local community pantries, while coffee grounds nourish community vegetable gardens across our quarter.
